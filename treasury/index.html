Why we built $X

From Real-World Businesses to a user-driven economy where customers and businesses grow together.

TaleX set out to build a user-driven ecosystem for Real-World Businesses, letting customers benefit from the growth of the businesses they buy from.

By featuring traditional consumer-oriented businesses as RWB projects, TaleX helps entrepreneurs boost their product sales while their customers share in the upside. Today the ecosystem spans ViewsCall, the YouTube prediction market; RoamFi, the eSIM that pays you back; and the TaleX content app, where great content pays off both creators and supporters.

To unlock further growth, we launched $X, the official native token of the TaleX ecosystem. Every dollar of company revenue buys $X on the open market and injects it back into the main $X liquidity pool — delivering value to the community and deepening the market every RWB project settles against.

Methodology

From 14 Sep 2026, "Revenue" includes all TaleX ecosystem projects' company revenue — ViewsCall, RoamFi and the TaleX content app — net of referral fees and cash backs. Fiat revenue is converted into USDT for use in purchasing $X. As of 14 Sep 2026, 100% of company revenue is allocated to buy $X on the open market and inject it into the main $X liquidity pool.
